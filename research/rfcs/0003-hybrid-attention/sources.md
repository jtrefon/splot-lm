# Primary-source notes: hybrid token mixing
Accessed 2026-11-26. Focused reading, not systematic; no external implementation copied or reproduced.
- Songlin Yang, Jan Kautz, Ali Hatamizadeh. *Gated Delta Networks: Improving Mamba2 with Delta Rule*, ICLR 2025, arXiv:2412.06464v3 (6 Mar 2025). [HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.06464v3), sections 3.1–3.4 and Appendix A formulation; Eq. 10 is the source for gated delta update (paper uses value-by-key orientation, SPLOT transposes). Hybrid variants in paper use sliding-window/Mamba2; SPLOT full GQA is a separate proposal. Their benchmark results are not SPLOT evidence.
- [Hugging Face Qwen3-Next implementation](https://github.com/huggingface/transformers/blob/main/src/transformers/models/qwen3_next/modeling_qwen3_next.py), main viewed 2026-11-26, selected recurrent update, chunk and GDN projection sections. Used for conventions (raw QKV convolution, L2 norm, scalar gates, grouped heads), not copied code. Mutable main URL not a reproducibility pin; pin commit and verify license before any integration. SPLOT equal head counts, gate choices and sizes are own proposal.
- Joshua Ainslie et al. *GQA: Training Generalized Multi-Query Transformer Models from Multi-Head Checkpoints*, EMNLP 2023, [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.298/), abstract and publication metadata; grouping proposal. No assumption their uptraining results apply to SPLOT.
- Jianlin Su et al. *RoFormer: Enhanced Transformer with Rotary Position Embedding*, [arXiv:2104.09864](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.09864), abstract/metadata. Standard pair rotation specified in RFC; interleaved convention is explicit.
- Ali Hatamizadeh, Yejin Choi, Jan Kautz. *Gated DeltaNet-2: Decoupling Erase and Write in Linear Attention*, [arXiv:2605.22791](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22791), abstract/metadata only (21 May 2026). Decouples erase/write with channel-wise gates; potential future ablation, not validated in SPLOT.
- Junsoo Oh, Jihun Yun, Junhyuck Kim, Hanbaek Lyu. *Integral DeltaNet: Improved Linear Attention with Adaptive Memory Integration*, [arXiv:2611.32063](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32063), abstract/metadata only (24 Nov 2026). Adds integral state alongside delta state; increases state and rollback contract. Future review, no performance claim.
- Kimi Delta Attention and alternative sparse/global schemes are follow-up leads; not independently reviewed here.

Full-text review of newer mixers, pinned kernel compatibility, license audit, end-to-end numerical replication remain outstanding.
