# Recurrent-depth primary-source notes
Accessed 2026-11-26. Targeted reading, not systematic review. Author-reported evidence only; no reproduction. Bibliographic metadata and relevant sections checked.

- Geiping, J., McLeish, S., Jain, N., Kirchenbauer, J., Singh, S., Bartoldson, B. R., Kailkhura, B., Bhatele, A., & Goldstein, T. (2025). *Scaling up Test-Time Compute with Latent Reasoning: A Recurrent Depth Approach*, arXiv:2502.05171v2 (17 Feb 2025). [Abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.05171v2), [HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.05171v2), §§3.1–3.3, particularly truncated backpropagation. Huginn uses a recurrent body and persistent prelude representation, with variable-depth training; main experiments truncate backward to the last eight iterations. SPLOT uses deterministic z initialization and full backward initially; not a reproduction. Their 3.5B/800B-token scale differs radically from our pilots.
- Huang, B., Shi, C., Chen, J., Wen, S., Liu, Z., Xing, E., & Ma, X. (2026). *Towards Looped Models Done Right, Part I: Topology, Input Injection, Recurrent-State Design*, arXiv:2610.23036v1 (16 Oct 2026). [Abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23036v1), [HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.23036v1), §§2,3.1,3.4. Controlled topology comparison supports sandwich; §3.4.2 reports improved context retrieval but lower MATH500/GSM8K in its prelude-injection comparison; random initialization and additional H/L states did not reliably help. Their scales and schedule differ; use injection ablations, not automatic adoption.
- He, D., Li, P., Chang, D., Meng, Q., Yin, L., & Liu, S. (2026). *Looping Beyond Twice: A Scalable Recipe for Looped Mixture-of-Experts*, arXiv:2610.01153v1 (1 Oct 2026). [Abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01153v1), [HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.01153v1), §§3.1–3.2 and §4.3 excerpt. LOOM scales residual branches with total loop depth, mixes embeddings back in, and uses distinct routers. We deliberately fix scale by config cap rather than runtime requested depth to preserve prefix compatibility; this is our own proposal and not validated by LOOM. Distinct routers and additional norm/loop residual are separate ablations.
- Zhu, R.-J., et al. (2025). *Scaling Latent Reasoning via Looped Language Models*, arXiv:2510.25741 (initial submission 29 Oct 2025; retrieved page includes 3 July 2026 manuscript). [Record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.25741), introduction/abstract checked. Ouro shares full stack and studies learned depth allocation; adaptive depth deferred in SPLOT. No claim of matching Ouro capability or scaling budget.

All records are preprints in the versions consulted; peer-review status not established here. Full literature review and implementation/license inspection remain open. Useful follow-up leads from LOOM references: SMELT arXiv:2609.01343, residual scaling arXiv:2606.18524 (not independently verified in this work).
